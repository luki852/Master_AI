Outline

• Manifolds: no global structure, need to work locally
• Basic notions in differential geometry allowing to formally define ”stuff” locally on
  manifolds (tangent spaces, Riemannian metric, exponential map) and ”move it
  around” (geodesics, parallel transport)
• Domain deformations and isometries (Riemannian and metric)
• Convolution-like operators on manifolds and the problem of gauge choice
• How to deal with ambiguity of local reference choice? (“gauge-equivariance”)

Takeaways

• No global structure on manifolds: “convolution = transform + match” is not well-
  defined
• Convolution-like operators must be defined locally (in the tangent space) and
  expressed w.r.t. some basis, which is defined up to a local transformation (“gauge
  choice”)
 • Invariant (symmetric) filters
