Takeaways

• Considering GNNs as discretised physical systems provides a different & sometimes
  more convenient perspective
• Many standard GNNs can be formalised as a discretised Graph Diffusion equation
• The physical perspective allows to make principled architectural choices (e.g., residual
  connection, shared symmetric weights) and provides theoretical guarantees (e.g.,
  asymptotic behaviour, expressive power, etc.)
• Though we have dealt only with diffusion-type equations, other physical models have
 also been successfully used in the Graph ML literature

• Diffusion equation (Euclidean, manifold, graph)
• Gradient flow
